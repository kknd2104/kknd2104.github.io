01 — AgentDrive

An AI-ready robot controller that behaves like industrial equipment.

AgentDrive is our onboard controller: mapping, localisation, planning, task execution and safety in one stack. It is already integrated into our robots and running in the field.

The language model plans. It never drives.

In AgentDrive an LLM never commands the motors directly. It designs a finite-state machine for the task; that state machine is checked and then executed deterministically by the controller. The result is the consistency and repeatability industrial sites expect, with no improvised motion.

  1. 1Task requestPlain language or Fleet OS job
  2. 2LLM designs an FSMStates, transitions, conditions
  3. 3FSM is checkedBefore anything moves
  4. 4Deterministic executionSame input, same behaviour
  5. 550 Hz control & safety loopLow-level, always on

05 — Safety & standards

Safety sits below the intelligence, not inside it.

AI decides what a robot should do next. It does not get a vote on whether the robot stops. Our safety functions run in a separate low-level layer that the planning software cannot override.

Planning

LLM-designed state machines, checked before execution. No direct motor commands from the model.

Execution

Deterministic FSM execution on AgentDrive for repeatable behaviour, cycle after cycle.

Control & safety loop · 50 Hz

An emergency stop, a safety-controller trip or a stalled wheel stops the robot immediately.

ISO 3691-4

Our mobile robots are designed to ISO 3691-4 requirements, the safety standard for driverless industrial trucks and their systems. The care humanoid adds a proximity safety layer of 24+ ToF sensors that works independently of its AI.
